Ember Field logos

Ember Field draws pictures out of thousands of glowing, swirling particle trails. It can output them in two ways: as a wallpaper made of pixels, or as a terminal logo made of braille characters, where each character is a tiny 2×4 grid of dots. These are the logos it made for your fastfetch.

The Flame logo next to your real fastfetch info. The full-size logos are 18 lines tall, exactly the height of the info column.

Mid-size logos

12 lines tall, two-thirds of the full height. Six new shapes, plus a few in other theme palettes.

Full-size logos

The swirls are poured into a silhouette, so you see a clear shape filled with flowing texture. Any outline works: a letter, an icon or a simple drawing.

Small logos

6 lines tall, the same size as the logo you use now.

Abstract logos

A wallpaper shrunk straight into dots. There's no picture of anything in them, only the swirl pattern itself. These are the ones from my first attempt.

Try them in your own terminal: ~/.config/fastfetch/logos/preview shows all of them, and ~/.config/fastfetch/logos/preview flame shows one.

To use one permanently, tell Claude its name. Your fastfetch config gets backed up first.
